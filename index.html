How it works

Three steps to publication-ready data.

Designed to minimize setup time and maximize clarity.

1

Upload

Drag and drop raw FastQ reads, qPCR machine files, plate-reader exports or data tables into your secure workspace.

~ 1 min
2

Configure

Pick a module, set your conditions and controls through a guided wizard. Smart defaults, no parameters to memorize.

~ 2 min
3

Explore & export

Play with interactive plots, check quality metrics, and download vector figures and processed tables instantly.

ready for your paper
Built for bench science

Go beyond the result. Trust it.

Easily validate the quality and reliability of your experiments — every analysis is reproducible by design.

Automated processing

Raw sequencing reads and instrument exports become structured results automatically. You do not need to set command-line flags or environments.

Publication figures

Interactive volcano plots, heatmaps and curves. Tune thresholds live, search your genes, export high-res vector graphics ready for submission.

Built-in quality control

Every run records its exact parameters, QC metrics and sample history: results are reproducible across your whole team.
